Brain audit

Instructions for your Claude Code. Copy, paste, say yes. A few minutes.

  1. Open the Claude app on your Mac. If it offers an update, update first.
  2. Top-left, next to the chat icon, click the </> icon (Code mode).
  3. Below the message box, click Project or folder and choose your Isracap-Brain folder (inside Google Drive).
  4. Press the Copy button below, paste into Claude's message box and press Enter.
  5. When Claude asks for permission to read files or run a command, answer Yes. The only file it writes is brain-audit.md.
  6. When it's done, send brain-audit.md to Meir on WhatsApp or by email.
The instructions are in English because Claude works best that way, and the report comes out in English for Meir. It leaves out names, emails and message content.
